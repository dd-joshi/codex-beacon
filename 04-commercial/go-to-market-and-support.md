# Go-to-market and customer support plan

> **Status:** Future scaled launch reference. Current acquisition and customer gates are the [Instagram plan](instagram-build-in-public-plan.md) and [solo-maker plan](../03-roadmap/solo-maker-lite-pro-plan.md); current technical architecture is the [software prototype plan](../03-roadmap/software-prototype-plan.md). Battery and power-system work and claims are outside this repository and require separate readiness evidence before any commercial unit.

## Launch objective

Launch Peri6 as a trusted creator tool, not a novelty desk gadget. The commercial story must show a real moment:

> Start a long render, walk away, and know when it finishes or needs you.

The launch optimizes for product truth, field learning, and controlled quality—not maximum day-one order volume.

## Launch cohorts

| Cohort | Approximate size | Purpose | Units |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Discovery partners | 15 | Behavior, visual language, pricing | Rough/concierge prototypes |
| Alpha partners | 8–12 | Architecture and assisted journey debugging | Alpha engineering units |
| EVT pilot | 15–20 active users | Real-workflow reliability and removal test | EVT units |
| Private DVT beta | 25–40 | Six-week field metrics and support rehearsal | Production-intent DVT units |
| Internal/PVT field simulation | Selected testers | Factory/release/packaging validation | PVT units, not promised stock |
| Customer canary | 100 | Acute field-quality containment | Final mass-production units |
| General first run | Remaining inventory | Public DTC release after G7 | Final released units |

Do not label EVT/PVT testing as a public commercial launch. Do not silently sell non-final units.

## Audience and channels

Initial channel: direct-to-consumer product site for the United States. The first audience is independent Windows video editors, motion designers, and 3D artists who repeatedly wait on long renders/exports.

Initial audience channels:

- creator workflow communities and newsletters;
- video/3D/developer educators with real long-task workflows;
- desk-setup media where practical value can be demonstrated;
- application-specific communities for verified integrations;
- founder/product development journal focused on calm computing and trust.

Avoid broad gamer RGB marketing that attracts the wrong expectation before the product’s behavior is understood.

India is the prototype/research base rather than the first volume forecast. Keep Indian beta evidence separate from US price conversion. Developers remain a secondary cohort until the creator workflow passes G1.

## Hero demonstration

A 15-second video should show:

1. A user starts a render/export/build.
2. Peri6 shifts from Calm to Working/Progress.
3. The user leaves the desk.
4. The task completes.
5. Peri6 shows one completion sweep and retains the soft marker.
6. The user returns, understands the result, and acknowledges it.

Secondary demonstrations:

- high utilization shown as normal useful work;
- sustained thermal throttling shown as attention with explanation;
- failed/cancelled distinct from success;
- focus timer;
- router loss/rejoin, ESP/desktop restart, stale-scene expiry, and local/account-free operation;
- a desktop pack/pattern update producing a new visual on unchanged compatible ESP firmware.

## Product-page structure

1. Emotional promise and hero journey
2. “Okay, working, done, or needs you” behavior
3. Completion memory
4. Supported desktop packs, exactly-one-active behavior, compatible ESP capabilities, and generic fallback
5. Local-first privacy and no mandatory subscription
6. Calm physical design and accessibility
7. Setup flow
8. Exact compatibility checker
9. Honest limitations and indeterminate-progress explanation
10. Warranty, returns, support period, shipping, and regulatory information

Do not lead with CPU percentages, LED specifications, customization, or app count.

## Claims policy

Every public claim maps to verification evidence.

Avoid broad promises such as:

- “tracks any render”;
- “supports every GPU”;
- “prevents overheating”;
- “always shows exact progress”;
- “zero setup”;
- “works everywhere.”

Prefer:

- explicit supported matrix;
- generic working/completion behavior where verified;
- exact progress for named versions/workflows only;
- measured setup and reliability claims;
- clear unsupported/unknown handling.

Reviewer materials must use the same truth. Review units ship only after support coverage and recovery paths are live.

Do not claim simultaneous pack monitoring, off-LAN control, battery life, charging behavior, portability, or power-system readiness in the current phase. Multiple entitlements mean a broader selectable desktop pack library; the ESP receives generic scenes and never an application or pack identity.

## Reservation and preorder policy

Discovery reservations should be refundable and used as demand evidence.

If commercial preorders are later accepted:

- disclose prototype/production status and realistic timing;
- state refund rules plainly;
- keep customer funds segregated or conservatively managed according to legal/accounting advice;
- do not promise worldwide compatibility or delivery;
- communicate material schedule/feature changes promptly;
- never use non-refundable orders to bypass failed evidence gates.

Kickstarter may amplify a later working product but cannot serve as problem discovery. Because India is not currently a directly supported creator country, any campaign/entity/payment structure requires qualified Indian and US advice after demand evidence. For commercial US fulfillment, obtain exact customs classification and use a planned batch import/US 3PL path rather than assuming low-value individual parcels will enter duty-free.

## Staged launch

### Canary

Ship 100 final units. For 7–10 days:

- review activation, same-LAN discovery/manual fallback, pairing/authentication, router loss/rejoin, IP change, ESP/desktop restart, pack compatibility, task outcomes, stale-scene expiry, updates, and support daily;
- quarantine remaining inventory if a common cause is suspected;
- classify every issue by severity and lot/configuration;
- operate a Product/Quality/Support decision room;
- publish corrections or known issues honestly.

### General release

Release remaining first-run inventory only after a written Gate G7 review confirms:

- no safety/systemic blocker;
- canary failures are contained;
- support load is manageable;
- return/RMA projection is acceptable;
- the release/update path works;
- contribution economics remain intact.

## Support experience

Support is part of product trust, not a post-launch department.

### In-product help

- “Why is this on?”
- Available/unsupported sensor explanation
- Compatibility report
- Connection/update/recovery status
- Active pack/version, exactly-one-active status, and pack enable/disable/update/rollback
- Privacy-redacted diagnostic export preview
- Local history inspection/deletion
- Installer repair and device recovery

### Public support assets

- Getting started
- Supported Windows versions and hardware
- Supported applications/workflows and exact behavior
- Meaning of every pattern
- Unknown/unavailable/disconnected states
- Same-private-LAN, guest/client-isolation, discovery/manual-address, pairing/reset, router loss/rejoin, and PC sleep/wake troubleshooting
- Installer/updater repair
- Firmware recovery
- Privacy/data behavior
- Warranty, return, replacement, recycling, and end-of-life

### Service targets

- First response within one business day
- Fewer than 10 support contacts per 100 shipped units after first month
- More than 80% of common setup issues resolved by self-diagnostics
- Customer satisfaction above 90% for resolved cases
- Structured reason for every return/RMA
- Early failures physically analyzed when practical

## Warranty and returns baseline

Provisional target, subject to launch-market law and funded economics:

- 30-day return period;
- two-year limited hardware warranty;
- at least five years of security/compatibility support from last sale;
- clear exclusions without hiding manufacturing defects;
- advance replacement where economically/logistically viable;
- 20–30 units or approved 2–3% launch replacement reserve.

Track defect, compatibility, expectation mismatch, buyer remorse, shipping damage, and no-longer-needed separately. A total return percentage without reason hides product problems.

## Field quality process

Use FRACAS/CAPA:

1. Receive field issue
2. Protect customer and contain affected lot/release
3. Classify severity and reproduce
4. Identify root cause
5. Correct product/process/documentation
6. Verify correction
7. Monitor recurrence
8. Close with evidence

Serial history should link hardware revision, component/PCBA lot, firmware, scene-protocol/capability version, desktop-host version, installed pack versions, calibration, factory test, shipment, and RMA finding.

## Incident playbooks

Prepare and rehearse:

- bad desktop application release;
- bad application-pack update, rollback, or stale event after a pack switch;
- failed firmware update or bricked device;
- local discovery/pairing outage, router/client-isolation failure, pairing-secret compromise, spoof/replay attempt, or desktop/ESP capability mismatch;
- unsupported Windows/driver/application update;
- false successful completion;
- abnormal false urgent alerts;
- common hardware/optical defect;
- overheating/electrical safety concern;
- privacy/security vulnerability or incident;
- delayed inventory or lost shipment;
- regulatory stop-sale/recall;
- abnormal return/review pattern.

## Post-launch decision

At 30 days, review:

- confidence-to-walk-away outcome;
- retained weekly use by mode;
- false/missed/failed task outcomes;
- activation, crashes, updates, recovery, support, returns, DOA/RMA;
- ratings and qualitative expectations;
- contribution margin and cash conversion;
- supplier/yield/field defect evidence.

Only then decide whether to reorder, correct V1, add packs, start macOS, harden the production radio path, explore off-LAN remote/cloud/mobile operation, or hold/stop.
